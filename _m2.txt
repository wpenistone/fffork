docs: P1.3 FileItem cannot shrink without breaking public APIs

Audited FileItem (48 B on Windows) field by field:

  size u64 (8)  modified u64 (8)  presence u64 (8)  path ChunkedString (8)
  parent_dir_index u32 (4)  git_status_bits u32 (4)
  access/modification/git_recency i16 x3 (6)  flags AtomicU8 (1)
  = 47 B, padded to 48.

Packing the tail does not help: the struct is 8-byte aligned, so
git_status_bits u32->u8 (the only private field) gives 44 B, which still
pads to 48. Getting to 40 needs a whole 8 B gone, i.e. one of
size/modified/presence narrowed, or the frecency scores halved to i8.

Every one of those is blocked:
- `size` and `modified` are u64 in the PUBLIC C struct (fff-c
  ffi_types.rs:138-139) and re-exposed by fff-python/fff-nvim/fff-mcp.
  AGENTS.md: top level rust/lua/C/bun APIs cannot change, ever.
- Moving them to a side table means accessor changes in all four binding
  crates, which is the same violation. `size` alone has 41 references and
  `git_status` 259, across 39 files.

So the largest single memory term in the index (10.9 MB at 227k files,
and the array the gate streams over on every query) is fixed at 48 B
under this project's own API stability rule.

What remains available without touching public API: nothing structural.
Remaining optimisation headroom is in the two already-measured stages -
the gate (4.2-5.5 ms) and SW (2.5-2.9 ms) on sumo_fork - not in layout.
