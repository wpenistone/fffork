## Findings

1. **Small corpora (311-10k)**: both sides ~0.1 ms. Speedup ratios are timer noise.
   RSS opt <= up. No real regression once out of 100us resolution.
   Caveat: opt showed 0.4-0.6x on a few cells at 0.0-0.1 ms — do not treat as signal.

3. **Adaptive collapse (sample-verified) did not trigger on `score`**.
   PHASE: prefilter 10ms + batch SW 6.5ms (full SW). Cheap rank and SW
   disagree on top-32 -> we keep SW ranking. This is intentional:
   wall is 2.8x vs upstream (not the earlier 9x from quality-blind collapse).
   Ranking quality wins over cherry-picked wall.
